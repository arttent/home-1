Background  
ARTTENT is a Georgian art management company based in Tbilisi, active across Georgia and internationally since 2019.

Website Purpose  
Promotes ARTTENT’s art initiatives and services, showcases artists and projects, and facilitates contact from collectors, institutions, and artists.

Design & Layout Preferences  
Clean, contemporary, art‑focused presentation.  
Artist roster: Maka Zedelashvili, Laka Popkhadze, Tamar Gedevanishvili, Maka Jishkariani, Elene Rakviashvili.  
Non-circular artist photos with names positioned in the lower left.  
Consistent sizing across all artist photos.  
Artist grid layout with Maka Jishkariani on a lower row relative to others.  
Artist order within grid: Maka Zedelashvili, then Laka Popkhadze, then Tamar Gedevanishvili, with others following; Laka positioned between Maka Zedelashvili and Tamar.  
Primary introductory/hero text aligned to the left and placed very close to the top edge.  
Logo placed on the right side of the page and much larger than initially (approximately three times the original size).  
Overall preference for sections (including “What we help you do”) to sit higher on the page with reduced vertical spacing.  

Technical / Structural Requirements  
Single-page marketing site.
